# Senior Software Engineer — Platform

**Company:** Northstar Labs  
**Location:** Remote (US)

## About the role
We are hiring a Senior Software Engineer to build and scale our customer-facing platform. You will own services end-to-end, improve reliability, and partner with product on roadmap delivery.

## Requirements
- 5+ years professional software engineering experience
- Strong proficiency in Python and TypeScript
- Experience designing and operating REST APIs and event-driven systems
- Hands-on experience with AWS (ECS/EKS, Lambda, S3, RDS)
- Familiarity with Docker, CI/CD, and infrastructure as code
- Solid understanding of relational databases and SQL performance
- Experience writing automated tests and improving observability

## Nice to have
- LangChain / LLM application experience
- Kubernetes production experience
- Prior work on applicant-tracking or HR-tech products

## Responsibilities
- Design, implement, and maintain backend services and APIs
- Improve system reliability, latency, and developer experience
- Collaborate with product and design on feature delivery
- Mentor teammates through code review and technical design
- Participate in on-call rotations and incident response
